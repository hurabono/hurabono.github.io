## Three Months Left in 2026

2026 ends in three months.

It's been the hardest year I've had. It also feels like a year where I didn't accomplish much, and somehow, at the same time, a year where I did.

![Screen capture from September 16, 2026](https://ik.imagekit.io/stephanie/%ED%99%94%EB%A9%B4%20%EC%BA%A1%EC%B2%98%202026-09-16%20151612.png)

Something about my mindset feels different now.

2026 was the year I threw everything I had into finding a job, and there were no jobs. I studied hard, I looked everywhere, and a full year went by with nothing to show for it.

And I mean there really is nothing. I looked, properly looked, and there are no jobs in my field. It's not just me either. Everyone says the same thing.

![Photo](https://ik.imagekit.io/stephanie/IMG_0099.jpeg)

Money leaves your account just for breathing, and a little bit of a date night takes a little more of it. I honestly don't know how I would have managed this year without my boyfriend. Thank you, seriously.

So yes, it's a rough patch. But shouldn't I be making at least a small opening for myself out of it? I've decided to borrow this time and put it toward one more certification.

I have AZ-900, so now I want to go for MD-102.

## A realistic plan

I've set this up as **8 to 10 weeks, sitting the exam in early December**.

The target is **6 to 8 hours a week**. I'm studying around a part-time job, so this pace leaves room to organize my notes properly and still take it at a reasonable speed. As a certification to close out 2026 with, that feels like enough.

Honestly, December is Christmas season and I know my attention will be somewhere else, so the strategy has to be getting all the foundational knowledge in place by early November and spending whatever's left purely on practice questions.

Booking the exam two weeks ahead matters. Seats disappear during the holiday rush.

People say MD-102 is harder than the exams I've taken before, so I did some digging for resources.

[MD-102 review thread on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/O365Certification/comments/1gi2gzc/md102_microsoft_365_certified_endpoint/)

The takeaway seems to be that not repeating the same mistakes is the whole game.

## Resources I've gathered

### Official exam page

[Microsoft 365 Certified: Endpoint Administrator Associate](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/modern-desktop/?practice-assessment-type=certification#two-ways-to-prepare)

The official page for MD-102. It covers pricing, helps with booking, and lays out the knowledge areas the exam expects.

### Endpoint Administrator exam resource guide

[MD-102 Endpoint Administrator Exam Resource Guide](https://intunedin.net/2024/09/09/md-102-endpoint-administrator-exam-resource-guide-july-2024-update/)

The last update looks like 2024, so I'll need to cross-check anything that may have changed since. Worth reading with that in mind.

### Free Intune trial

[Sign up for a free trial and configure a Microsoft Intune tenant](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/intune/fundamentals/free-trial-sign-up)

Intune is supposed to be the core of MD-102. There's a one-month free trial, and using it to build out tickets would get me two things at once.
